# Business Requirements Document (BRD)
## Compliance Assistant for Banking & Financial Services

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** August 2025  
**Prepared for:** Chief Operating Officer   

## 1. Executive Summary
### Business Problem
Our banking and financial services organization faces significant operational challenges in managing IT security and data privacy compliance obligations. Currently, business analysts spend excessive manual effort creating and maintaining obligation matrices, creating operational inefficiencies and audit risks. With an upcoming audit in 6-12 months, we need an automated solution to ensure 100% coverage of compliance obligations and reduce the risk of compliance and audit failures.

### Proposed Solution
The Compliance Assistant is an intelligent document analysis system that automatically extracts, analyzes, and tracks compliance obligations from our policy documents. This solution will transform our manual compliance process into an automated, proactive system that ensures comprehensive obligation coverage and significantly reduces manual analyst workload to focus on higher value activities.

## 2. Business Objectives and Success Metrics
### Primary Objectives
- Automate Obligation Matrix Creation: Replace manual analyst work with intelligent document processing
- Ensure Audit Compliance: Achieve 100% coverage of compliance obligations
- Reduce Operational Risk: Minimize human error in compliance tracking
- Optimize Resource Allocation: Redirect analyst time to higher-value activities

### 3. Functional Requirements
#### Core Capabilities
- Intelligent Document Analysis
- Automatically process IT security and data privacy policy documents
- Extract compliance obligations and requirements
- Identify regulatory framework mappings
- Provide source citations and regulatory references
- Automated Obligation Matrix Generation
- Create comprehensive obligation matrices from policy documents
- Export to Excel/spreadsheet formats
- Maintain audit trails for all obligations identified

Future state:
- Proactive Compliance Monitoring
- Alert users to new or updated obligations
- Identify potential compliance gaps
- Provide recommendations for policy updates

### User Experience Requirements
- Business Analyst and COO Interface: Detailed obligation management and matrix generation
- Auditor Access: Read-only access to compliance documentation and evidence
